Execution Protocol

Continuation must be interrupted
before irreversible execution
becomes procedural default.

Systems are designed to continue under momentum. Interruption requires separate institutional structure.

Step 01

Trigger Detection

Execution enters interruption state when irreversible exposure and uncertainty coexist beyond predefined operational tolerance.

Irreversible propagation detected
+
Unresolved uncertainty present
=
Interruption condition triggered
Step 02

Automatic Pause

Continuation does not proceed automatically once interruption conditions are active. Execution enters temporary suspension state pending authorization review.

Continuation Status: PAUSED
Execution Authority: SUSPENDED
Propagation: RESTRICTED
Step 03

Named Override

Continuation requires explicit authorization by a named individual with recognized override authority.

Required Fields:

Name
Role
Timestamp
Continuation Scope
Authorization Basis
Step 04

Escalation

If no authorization is issued within escalation limits, continuation remains blocked and authority escalates automatically.

No Override Received
↓
Continuation Frozen
↓
Escalation Triggered
Step 05

Institutional Memory

Irreversible continuation events become part of permanent authorization history.

Who authorized continuation
When authorization occurred
Under what uncertainty conditions
What execution scope continued
