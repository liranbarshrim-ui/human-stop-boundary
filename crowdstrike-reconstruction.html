Authority Reconstruction · #02

CrowdStrike
The Propagation Window

04:09 — 05:27 UTC · July 19, 2024

This is not a reconstruction of the software defect.
It is a reconstruction of interruption authority during propagation under trusted deployment.

01 — Deployment
04:09 UTC · July 19, 2024

Routine update.
Automatic propagation begins.

CrowdStrike distributed a sensor configuration update, Channel File 291, to Windows systems running its Falcon endpoint security platform. The update was part of routine operations, distributed automatically to customers who had configured their systems to receive updates as released.

CrowdStrike's Falcon platform was deployed across approximately 24,000 customers, including nearly 60% of Fortune 500 companies. The update required no customer action to install.

Documentary Basis

At 04:09 UTC on July 19, CrowdStrike released a sensor configuration update for Windows hosts as part of ongoing operations. The update contained a logic error that caused an out-of-bounds memory read, resulting in system crashes across affected machines.

Source: CrowdStrike Preliminary Post Incident Review, July 24, 2024 · CrowdStrike Root Cause Analysis, August 6, 2024
02 — Propagation
04:09 — 05:27 UTC

Systems crash.
Propagation continues.

Within minutes, systems receiving the update began crashing. Affected machines entered a boot loop or recovery state, unable to restart normally without manual intervention on each device.

Because the update deployed automatically, propagation did not require any affirmative action by customer organizations. Receiving the update was the default state. Interrupting propagation required explicit, coordinated action before or during distribution.

Documentary Basis

Approximately 8.5 million Windows devices were affected. Because many systems had to be remediated manually, restoring full operations was expected to take days. Systems that had already received the update before CrowdStrike identified and reverted the file could not be automatically recovered.

Source: Microsoft estimate, July 20, 2024 · CrowdStrike Preliminary Post Incident Review, July 24, 2024
03 — Reversion
05:27 UTC

CrowdStrike identifies
and reverts.

At 05:27 UTC, CrowdStrike identified the logic error and reverted Channel File 291. For systems that had not yet received the update, the reversion was effective. For systems that had already installed the defective file, reversion did not restore function. Manual remediation was required on each affected machine.

The structural condition this reconstruction examines is not the defect itself, but the propagation window between 04:09 and 05:27 UTC — 78 minutes during which the update continued to reach customer systems automatically.

Propagation was the default.
Interruption required explicit action
that most customers were not positioned to take.

05 — Structural Observation

What existed.
What remained unclear.

EXISTSTechnical interruption capability — customers could configure staged rollouts or delayed update acceptance
EXISTSPost-incident remediation — CrowdStrike and Microsoft provided recovery guidance
UNCLEARWho was named in advance to carry interruption authority before systems became unreachable
