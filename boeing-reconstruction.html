Authority Reconstruction · #01

Boeing 737 MAX
The Refusal Window

29 Oct 2018 — 13 Mar 2019

This is not a reconstruction of blame.
It is a reconstruction of authority under unresolved uncertainty.

01 — Signal
29 Oct 2018

Lion Air Flight 610.
The signal emerges.

The crash into the Java Sea resulted in 189 fatalities. Investigators identified MCAS — the Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System — as a significant contributing factor.

FAA's Continued Operational Safety process was triggered. The fleet remained in service.

02 — Quantified Risk
Nov – Dec 2018

TARAM.
Risk becomes a number.

FAA engineers conducted quantitative risk analyses. The Transport Airplane Risk Assessment Methodology projected, according to FAA internal modeling, approximately 15 future fatal crashes over the life of the fleet if the identified condition remained unresolved.

Risk was not unknown. Risk was not unexamined. Risk was measured.

Documentary Basis

FAA's Corrective Action Review Board reviewed the results of Lion Air TARAM risk analyses and evaluated corrective actions and implementation timelines.

Source: DOT OIG Report No. AV2023025, April 26, 2023 (Exhibit D)
03 — Institutional Review
Nov 7 · Nov 14 · Nov 28 · Dec 12, 2018

CARB convenes.
Oversight is present.

The Corrective Action Review Board met multiple times. An Emergency Airworthiness Directive was issued on November 7. A corrective action plan was evaluated and accepted. Boeing was assigned a timeline for software remediation.

Oversight existed. Escalation existed. Documentation existed.

Documentary Basis

FAA issued an emergency airworthiness directive on November 7 that directed operators of the MAX to revise their airplane flight manuals. This AD was an interim action designed to mitigate risk until Boeing could develop and implement a software update.

Source: DOT OIG Report No. AV2023025, April 26, 2023
04 — Continuation Logic
Dec 2018 — Mar 2019

Continuation
acquires documented justification.

Following the December 2018 CARB review, FAA accepted a corrective action plan and implementation timeline. The fleet remained operational while remediation was expected to proceed.

This is the structural condition this reconstruction examines: not the absence of process, but the presence of documented continuation logic.

Continuation was not accidental.
It was institutionally justified.
Documentary Basis

FAA followed its established processes following the Lion Air accident, including completing quantitative assessments, convening a technical board to evaluate corrective actions, and coming to an agreement with Boeing regarding a timeframe for implementing those corrective actions.

Source: DOT OIG Report No. AV2023025, April 26, 2023
05 — The Refusal Question

Once continuation proceeded
under an accepted remediation timeline,
who carried refusal?

This is not a question about intent. Not about capability. Not about whether individuals understood the risk.

It is a structural question:

Who carried refusal — by name?
