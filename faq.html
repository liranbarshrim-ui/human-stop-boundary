Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Asked When
Responsibility Is Real

Common questions from boards, founders, operators, counsel, and leaders facing consequential decisions already in motion.

If no one can answer now,
the authority gap is already active.
01
What is DAR?
A written record naming one individual with explicit authority to stop a specific irreversible decision before execution begins.
02
Who usually needs this?
Boards, executives, AI teams, regulated operators, founders, and organizations where multiple people approve but no one clearly owns the final stop decision.
03
Does this apply to AI systems?
Yes. If systems move faster than governance can respond, human control may become symbolic unless one named person can halt continuation in time.
04
What counts as irreversible?
Any decision whose consequences cannot be undone quickly enough to prevent material harm: deployment at scale, live automation, filings, public releases, critical transactions.
05
Approval authority vs stop authority?
Approval authority permits continuation. Stop authority prevents continuation until explicit release.
06
What if the board already approved it?
Approval does not always solve live execution risk. Conditions can change after approval. Real stop authority may still be required.
07
Who is exposed if failure happens?
Often the most visible signatory, executive owner, director, or function leader—whether or not they held usable authority in practice.
08
Is this legal advice?
No. This is independent advisory work. Legal matters should be handled by qualified counsel.
