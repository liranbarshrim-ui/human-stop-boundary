Frequently Asked Questions

Answers on Authority, Risk, and Irreversible Decisions

Common questions from boards, founders, operators, counsel, and leaders facing consequential decisions.

If stop authority already exists,
you should be able to name it immediately.
01
What is DAR — Decision Authority Record?
DAR is a written record naming one individual with explicit authority to stop a specific irreversible decision before execution. It is clarity in advance.
02
Who needs this?
Boards, executives, AI teams, regulated operators, counsel, and organizations where critical decisions can move without one clearly named stop owner.
03
Does this apply to AI systems?
Yes. If systems act faster than governance can respond, human control may become symbolic unless one person can halt continuation in time.
04
What counts as irreversible?
Any decision whose consequences cannot be undone quickly enough to prevent material harm: deployment at scale, execution thresholds, filings, public releases, live automation.
05
Approval authority vs stop authority?
Approval authority allows continuation. Stop authority blocks continuation until explicit release.
06
Why not let committees handle it?
Committees review. In critical moments, time compresses. Someone must personally hold usable authority now.
07
Is this legal advice?
No. This is independent advisory work. Legal matters should be handled by qualified legal counsel.
08
How long does a review take?
Typical focused reviews begin with initial findings within days, depending on urgency and scope.
