Authority Reconstruction · #04

Toyota Andon
Interruption by Design

Toyota Production System · A reconstructed interruption moment

This is not a reconstruction of a failure.
It is a reconstruction of interruption authority that worked.

01 — Activation

An operator notices.
The cord is pulled.

An operator on the assembly line observes a defect or an unexpected condition. The source is not yet understood. The cause is unresolved.

The Andon cord is pulled.

The line begins to slow.

Institutional Basis

Any line worker holds the authority — and the obligation — to pull the Andon cord when uncertainty is observed. The authority is not delegated in response to an event. It is assigned structurally before any event occurs.

Source: Ohno, T. (1988). Toyota Production System. Productivity Press.
02 — Signal

The line slows.
The Team Leader is notified.

When the cord is pulled, the line enters a buffer period. A visual and audible signal activates. The Team Leader is alerted to the affected station.

The source of the problem has not been diagnosed. The uncertainty is unresolved. Continuation has not resumed.

The signal does not wait for understanding. It precedes it.

03 — Interruption Before Understanding

Continuation paused
under unresolved uncertainty.

If the issue is not resolved before the buffer expires, the line stops.

Interruption did not require diagnosis to be complete. Interruption preceded understanding.

Continuation required explicit release.
Interruption did not require permission.
Institutional Basis

Production halts at the point of uncertainty. The Team Leader responds to the station — not to a committee, not after escalation. The person who stopped is not penalized. Stopping is the correct response.

Source: Liker, J.K. (2004). The Toyota Way. McGraw-Hill.
04 — Release

Team Leader arrives.
Continuation requires explicit release.

The Team Leader reaches the affected station. The condition is examined.

If the issue is understood and resolved, the Team Leader explicitly releases continuation. The line restarts.

If the issue cannot be resolved, the line remains stopped. The Team Leader does not resume continuation under uncertainty.

Silence does not authorize restart.

05 — The Interruption Question

Who carried interruption
authority — by name?

The same question posed to Boeing, CrowdStrike, and Knight Capital.

Here, the answer existed before the event.

The operator who pulled the cord.
Named in advance. By role. By obligation.
06 — Structural Observation

What was named.
What was designed.

NAMEDInterruption authority — any operator, by role and obligation, before any specific event
NAMEDRelease authority — Team Leader, explicitly, before continuation resumes
NAMEDPhysical mechanism — cord or button, accessible at the point of uncertainty
NAMEDCultural protection — the person who stopped is not penalized
EXISTSSilence protocol — non-response does not authorize continuation
EXISTSDocumentation — interruption and restart recorded as production data

Interruption authority was named before any event occurred.
Continuation required explicit release.

Interruption came first.
Continuation required release.
