Clarity Supports Judgment.
It Does Not Replace It.

Matrix Audit frameworks are designed to help organizations clarify responsibility before consequential decisions become irreversible.

A framework can define accountability.
Only people can exercise judgment.

Purpose

Decision Authority Research is a governance methodology intended to clarify authority, escalation, and accountability before critical decisions occur.

Scope

It may align with oversight, governance, and regulatory expectations, but it is not a legal opinion, compliance certification, or guarantee against liability.

Responsibility

Responsibility for decisions remains with the organization, its officers, board members, and designated decision makers.

Core Principle

DAR can identify who should be accountable to stop. It does not make the decision. A named human being does.

Good Faith Standard

Any use of these materials should be honest, lawful, proportionate, and directed toward real governance improvement rather than symbolic optics.

Practical Reality

Documents do not stop events. Authority exercised in time does.
