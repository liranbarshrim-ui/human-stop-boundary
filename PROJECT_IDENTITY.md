# Project Identity

## Canonical project name

**Decision Authority Record (DAR)**

## Framework / review layer

**DAR** is the project's named-authority governance construct and record. The **Decision Authority Record** identifies, in advance, who holds refusal/stop authority before an irreversible effect.

Where the project uses a review or audit process to examine such records and their enforcement boundary, that review process may be described as a **DAR review** or **DAR Boundary Audit**. **Decision Accountability Review** is not the canonical expansion of DAR.

## Naming rule

Use:

- **DAR** → Decision Authority Record
- **DAR review / DAR Boundary Audit** → the review or audit process applied to the record and its enforcement boundary
- **Human Stop Boundary** → the enforcement-boundary implementation/research prototype
- **Decision Accountability Review** → historical / non-canonical wording only; do not use it as the current expansion of DAR

## Why this distinction matters

The repository contains governance records, review/audit material, and an implementation boundary. Keeping the record, review process, and implementation clearly named makes provenance easier to audit and reduces ambiguity in citations, licensing notices, research references, and external review.

## Historical names

Git history contains earlier naming variants, including periods in which different DAR expansions were used. Historical commits remain part of the immutable repository record and are not rewritten merely to normalize current terminology.

**Canonical as of the current repository:** Decision Authority Record (DAR).
