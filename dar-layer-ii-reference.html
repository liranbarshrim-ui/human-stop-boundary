Layer II asks: Is the name portable?
Classification model — provisional  ·  Working model v0.2
Layer I — Original
Who, by name, is authorized to refuse continuation before a decision becomes irreversible?
Layer II — New
If that person disappeared tomorrow — where would refusal authority live?
PERSON
Authority lives in individual judgment, relationships, or memory. Not transferable. Disappears with the person.
ROLE
Authority attached to the title. Survives — but only if explicitly scoped and documented as belonging to the role, not the individual.
STRUCTURE
Authority written into governance artifacts. Named, bounded, with an explicit continuity mechanism. Survives absence automatically.
UNLOCATED
Authority could not be located in person, role, or documented structure. No recoverable authority anchor could be identified.
