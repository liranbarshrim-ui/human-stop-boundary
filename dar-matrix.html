Who Holds Refusal Authority?

26 real-world and emerging cases testing one operational question: after refusal, can execution proceed anyway? A binary test yields three operational forms. Confidence levels reflect evidence quality, not ideological commitment.

Three N/A cases are excluded because the failure mode is epistemic rather than authority-related.

Level 1
Advisory Refusal
Continuation proceeds. Objection is documented but not binding. Named individual may flag, challenge, or escalate.
Test: System continues after objection is raised. → Continuation unaffected.
Level 2
Conditional Refusal Authority
Continuation pauses. Override possible through committee or board. Refusal is procedural friction, not a boundary.
Test: Continuation resumes after escalation without resolving the refusal. → Friction, not boundary.
Level 3
Independent Refusal Authority
Continuation cannot proceed. Resumption requires explicit release from the refusing authority or structural resolution.
Test: Continuation cannot proceed without resolving the refusal itself. → Boundary.
Operative Test — Single Question
After refusal, can execution proceed anyway?
Yes → Level 1 or 2.   No → Level 3.

The domain changes.
The velocity changes.
The question does not.

Across sectors, approval appears designed.
Refusal often appears improvised.

Key Finding — Post Taxonomy
  • Approval authority is usually explicit. Refusal authority often defaults to escalation.
  • L3 appears most consistently where irreversibility is salient and refusal authority is operationally or institutionally enforced
  • L2 appears as a common observed pattern in mature regulated environments — veto exists, but appeal path remains open
  • L1 is the dominant observed pattern across AI/agentic cases examined here
  • CRO veto (banking) is L2, not L3 — the appeal path to Board is the distinguishing feature
  • Challenger is L1 — refusal authority existed informally, was then actively removed under pressure
  • The question is binary. The observed answers are not.
Open Questions — Field Verification
  • When does a Conditional Refusal Authority (L2) become operationally equivalent to L3?
  • Can L3 be institutionalized in digital systems without a physical mechanism equivalent to the andon cord or PAL?
  • Does SMCR personal liability create de facto L3 behavior without formal L3 structure?
  • NHS Caldicott Guardian: L2 or L3? Requires field verification.
  • Is the L1/L2/L3 distinction testable in a structured interview with a named DPO or CRO?
Working Hypothesis
Independent refusal authority is most consistently observed in domains where irreversibility is salient and refusal authority is operationally or institutionally enforced.
Not all veto authority constitutes refusal authority. A veto that can be routinely overridden through escalation functions as procedural friction. Refusal authority exists when continuation cannot proceed without resolving the refusal.
