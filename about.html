About the work

A structural question. Nothing more.

Matrix Audit examines whether refusal authority actually binds continuation when a decision approaches an irreversible point.

The method

Identify the person. Define the process and stop condition. Trace what happens after refusal. Test whether continuation can bypass that refusal.

The distinction

Approval is not refusal.
Escalation is not refusal.
Oversight is not refusal.
Presence is not authority.

What DAR does not claim

DAR does not claim that every system can be stopped by a human, that a named authority automatically has real power, or that a framework can control arbitrary AI systems outside its enforcement boundary.

It is a structural review, not a mandate, certification, legal opinion, or maturity score. Strong claims apply only within the defined enforcement boundary being reviewed.

The work

The research separates concepts that are often treated as interchangeable, then examines the point at which their functions diverge. Evidence is drawn from documented governance failures and technical research. The objective is not to assign blame; it is to make the missing question visible before the event.
