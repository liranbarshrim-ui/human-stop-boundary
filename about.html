About

Liran Bar‑Shrim

Independent research focused on authority gaps, executive accountability, and the moment institutions discover too late that responsibility was assigned before authority was clear.

Core View

Most systems know how to approve. Fewer know how to stop.

Organizations build workflows, committees, escalation paths and controls. Yet when pressure rises, timelines compress, or consequences become personal, true stop authority is often unclear.

That gap can remain invisible until it matters most.

Why Independent Matters

Distance can reveal what proximity normalizes. Outside internal politics and routine assumptions, structural blind spots are often easier to identify.

Private Inquiry

For boards, executives, founders, General Counsel, CROs and leaders facing live matters where authority, accountability or intervention power is unclear.
