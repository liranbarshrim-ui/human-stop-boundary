Independent Research · Decision Authority

Focused on authority gaps, executive accountability, and the missing question many institutions discover too late: who can personally stop momentum when risk rises.

Core View

Most systems define motion better than restraint.

Organizations build approval paths, workflows and delegation models. Far fewer define real intervention authority once pressure, speed and uncertainty are already present.

If no one is clearly named to stop it, clarity often arrives after consequences.
Why Independent

Distance can reveal what proximity normalizes.

Inside complex systems, momentum can look like progress. Ownership diffuses, symbolic controls replace usable authority, and structural blind spots become routine.
