Focus

Stop authority. If no one is named in advance to stop a decision, the system does not actually know how to stop.

When a decision becomes irreversible under uncertainty, someone must have the authority to stop it — by name.

The question

Who is explicitly named, in advance, to stop a decision before it becomes irreversible?

Not a role. Not a team. A name.

Core principle

Stop is the default state. Continuation does not exist until explicitly released by a named individual bound to refusal. No release, no continuation. If continuation is available without that release, there is no stop.

Structural law

If no individual is explicitly defined in advance to stop a decision at the point of irreversibility, the system will assign a name after the outcome.

Applies to

Fintech. Clinical governance. Aviation. Critical infrastructure. Agentic AI. Any system where an irreversible decision can be reached without a named stop authority.

What this is not
A regulatory certification
Legal advice
A software product
A compliance framework
