# Origin & Public Timeline
### Named Stop Authority — Liran Bar-Shrim

---

## Accurate Claim

To the best of publicly available documented search, this is an early and distinct public formulation of **Named Stop Authority** as a governance model for irreversible decisions under uncertainty.

The term and concept — as a specific, named, pre-designated human authority to halt irreversible decisions before execution — does not appear in prior governance literature, AI safety frameworks, or regulatory documents in this formulation.

---

## What Existed Before

Adjacent concepts existed:

- **Human-in-the-loop** — a person is present in the process
- **Accountability frameworks** — who is responsible after the fact
- **Escalation paths** — how decisions move up the chain
- **RACI matrices** — who owns, who is informed
- **Audit trails** — what happened and when

None of these asked the specific question this framework asks:

> *Who, by name, is designated in advance to stop the decision before it becomes irreversible?*

---

## Public Timeline

### Stage 1 — Initial Public Formulation
**Late 2024 / Early 2025**

Early posts introducing the gap between automated decision-making and personal human responsibility.  
Core observation: responsibility cannot remain abstract when decisions are irreversible.  
Published on LinkedIn under Decision Authority Research / Matrix Audit.

---

### Stage 2 — Shift From Oversight to Authority
**Early 2025**

Public distinction between passive oversight and real stop power.  
Key formulation: review after the fact is insufficient. Authority must exist before the moment of execution.

---

### Stage 3 — Named Human Boundary
**Early 2025**

Introduction of the requirement for a named individual, designated in advance.  
Key formulation: not a team. Not a role. Not a system. A person. With a name.

---

### Stage 4 — Trigger Logic Defined
**Early–Mid 2025**

The boundary linked to two conditions: **irreversibility** and **material uncertainty**.  
Both conditions must be present for Named Stop Authority to apply.  
This distinguishes ordinary governance from the specific problem Named Stop Authority addresses.

---

### Stage 5 — Consolidated Governance Thesis
**Mid 2025 — Ongoing**

Named Stop Authority presented as a practical governance threshold for AI-era decisions.  
Published as a working paper, white paper, and multiple public essays at matrix-audit.com.  
Active engagement with governance professionals, compliance officers, and AI governance researchers on LinkedIn.

## What This Is Not

This is not a patent claim.  
This is not a claim of exclusive ownership over ideas of human accountability.  
This is not a claim that no one else has thought about stop mechanisms.

This is an early documented public formulation of a specific governance concept — the requirement for a **named individual, designated in advance, with protected authority to halt an irreversible decision** — as a distinct and necessary governance threshold.
