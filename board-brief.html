Board Brief
Named Refusal Authority
Decision Authority Research · Liran Bar-Shrim · 2026

If irreversible execution proceeds today, who by name holds the institutional right to refuse continuation before outcomes become permanent?

If this question cannot be answered with a name, refusal authority is unassigned. Continuation is already authorized by default.
The Gap
Most boards can name who approves. Almost none can name who holds refusal authority.

Organizations invest heavily in approval authority. Budget sign-off, risk committee review, board oversight.

Refusal authority is different. The question is not who approved continuation. The question is who, by name, holds the institutional right to refuse continuation before irreversible execution proceeds.

Where no named refusal authority exists, continuation proceeds by default.
Structure
Named refusal authority requires three distinct roles.
01
Refusal
Named in advance. Holds the institutional right to deny continuation.
02
Hold
Maintains suspension while governance responds.
03
Authorization
Separate named authority required to resume continuation.
Board Exposure
Where personal accountability concentrates.

When irreversible execution produces harm and no named refusal authority existed beforehand, accountability often concentrates after the fact.

If authorization cannot be assigned by name, continuation becomes institutional momentum rather than governance.
