Genesis

A chronological record of artifacts associated with the development of this work.

This page documents the earliest currently identified artifacts associated with the development of the concepts examined in this research. Entries are presented in documentary form without interpretive claims.

Where a date is uncertain or an artifact predates the current formulation, this is stated explicitly. This record will be updated as earlier artifacts are identified. All timestamps are drawn from verifiable sources where possible.

Phase 1

Origins and Precursors

These artifacts predate the DAR formulation. They explored related questions under different frameworks and terminology. They are recorded here as precursor artifacts, not as equivalent to the current work.
