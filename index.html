The Problem

Most organizations define how decisions proceed.
Almost none define who can stop them.

When a decision becomes irreversible under pressure, the absence of named stop authority is not a governance gap. It is personal exposure waiting to be assigned.

Approval systems scale. The person who carries failure does not.

The Framework

Decision Authority Record

DAR defines one named individual with explicit authority to stop a specific decision before it becomes irreversible. Not a committee. Not a policy. A person, named in advance, with a documented mandate.

01 — Pathway
Map how it moves
Identify how one consequential decision actually travels through your organization. Not how it should. How it does.
02 — Authority
Name one person
Define one accountable individual with real stop power. Named. Documented. Bound to the decision before exposure begins.
03 — Threshold
Define the moment
Clarify exactly when continuation must pause. What signal triggers review. What makes proceeding a named decision.
04 — Record
Create the DAR
Produce a formal written authority record before the decision point arrives. The document that changes who carries the consequence.
Stop authority without review becomes veto.
Review without stop becomes theater.
