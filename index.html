The Problem

Most organizations know how to approve decisions.
Few know who can stop them.

When pressure rises and a decision becomes irreversible, committees diffuse ownership, systems continue by inertia, and responsibility often lands later on one exposed individual.

The Framework

Decision Authority Record

DAR identifies one named person with explicit authority to pause or stop a specific decision before exposure becomes personal.

01

Map Reality

Trace how one consequential decision actually moves through the organization — not how policy says it should move.

02

Name Authority

Define one accountable human authority with real stop power. Not a committee. Not a mailbox.

03

Define Threshold

Clarify what uncertainty, escalation or irreversibility triggers mandatory pause and review.

04

Create Record

Produce a formal authority record that exists before the critical moment arrives.

Stop authority without review becomes veto.
Review without stop becomes theater.
