Private Governance Advisory

Authority must exist
before exposure begins.

When decisions become irreversible under uncertainty, responsibility lands on a person. DAR addresses that before blame, regulators or headlines do.

Confidential Inquiry
Most organizations define how decisions proceed. Almost none define who can stop them.
01 — Structure

Name one authority

One real person with explicit stop power before the decision crosses the line.

02 — Timing

Define the threshold

Clarify the moment continuation requires pause, review or human override.

03 — Protection

Reduce exposure

Separate collective approval theater from genuine personal accountability.

04 — Advisory

Selective engagements

Boards, founders, executives and counsel facing live high-stakes situations.
