Insights

Ideas that pressure usually hides.

Who resumes?

Whoever feels the pain of stopping. When no one is named to resume, pain becomes authority.

Approval Is Common

Most organizations know how to approve decisions. Few define who can stop them before damage compounds.

Silence Decides

When no one explicitly owns the decision, momentum often decides in their place.

Blame Arrives Late

Responsibility without authority often looks orderly until failure makes the gap public.
