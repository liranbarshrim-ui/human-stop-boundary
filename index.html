The Problem

Most organizations define how decisions proceed. Almost none define who can stop them.

Approval scales easily. Personal accountability does not. When pressure rises, unclear authority becomes personal exposure.

01

Map Reality

Trace how one consequential decision actually moves through the organization — not how policy says it should move.

02

Name Authority

Define one accountable human authority with real stop power. Not a committee. Not a mailbox.

03

Define Threshold

Clarify what uncertainty, escalation or irreversibility triggers mandatory pause and review.

04

Create Record

Produce a written authority record before the critical moment arrives — while choice still exists.

Why It Matters

Responsibility without authority is not governance.

It is delayed blame. DAR helps organizations define authority before failure forces the issue publicly.
