The Problem

Most organizations assign responsibility.
Few define who can stop.

Organizations are designed to move decisions forward. Approval paths, escalation chains, review committees — all built for continuation.

The harder function is deciding who can interrupt a decision already in motion, on named authority, within the window that matters.

When something goes wrong, accountability converges to a name.

Silent executive exposure is not the absence of responsibility. It is the absence of a named stop.
